Saideira

O que era sol agora é noite E o que era folia agora é sossego E o que era areia agora é fogueira E o que era brisa agora é arrepio

O que era roda agora é dupla O que era olhar desviado agora é toque

O que era praia agora—estrelas.

Derivado de O Churrasco.

Becoming a Poet

Walking my dog, I hear a podcast host ask his guest: “When did you become a poet?”

I mull over how to uncover a poet. Is the horny teenager trying to get into Patricia’s pants, his verses filled with red roses and blue violets, a poet? The caffeinated student flunking math, sentences enjambed with equations and sorrows? Gina, drying her tears between the lines, while searching for words that rhyme with hole—her boyfriend drooling over Patricia? My father-in-law reciting poems about men from Nantucket? Must poets write sonnets? Perhaps you’re finally a poet when someone wonders “When did you become a poet?”

I forgot the poop bag.

First published in Rat’s Ass Review, Fall–Winter 2026.
